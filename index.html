PhyloVista demo

A fast, ARB-style phylogenetic tree explorer that runs entirely in your browser. Fold large microbial trees by taxonomy, annotate clades, search and highlight by metadata, compare taxonomies and export figures — on trees as large as the full GTDB bacterial tree.

Things to try (5 minutes)

  1. Open the Archaea dataset. It starts folded to phylum. Right-click a phylum → Expand to order; the rest stays folded.
  2. Press / and search g__Methanobrevibacter; press Enter to jump there.
  3. Search checkm2_completeness>=95 genome_size<1500000 and press Shift+Enter to highlight the matches; use ⊙ in the Highlights panel to focus on them.
  4. Open the genomes table, filter it, and try Highlight each value of… ncbi_country.
  5. Look at the trait strips on the right: hover a cell; fold a clade and see its summary. Open strips to add or remove columns, and try the RED button.
  6. Right-click a clade → Create group…, give it a name and colour. Then Export ▾ → SVG for a figure.
  7. Open the NCBI vs GTDB dataset and look at the monophyly report: click highlight next to a split taxon.
  8. If you have your own tree (Newick) and a table of genomes (TSV/CSV, or a GTDB-Tk summary), try them with Open tree… and Load data….

Quick reference

Click: select · Double-click: fold/unfold

Shift+click: expand to the next rank

Alt/Option+click: distance to the selection

Right-click: menu (expand to rank, groups, root, export…)

Drag: pan · Wheel: zoom · Shift+wheel: zoom width

f fit · / search · g group · ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z undo
